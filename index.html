Why Osthelia?

Let's make things better.

Osthelia started as one person refusing to accept that this is just how esport has to be. Today it's a handful of us, building the alternatives we wish already existed: free, easy to use, open to everyone, and shaped by the communities that use them - not built to squeeze money out of them.

We can't fix everything. But we can build our own corner of it, the way it should have been from the start.

I got tired of watching the esport I love turn into a machine for money, drama, and bullshit. So instead of just complaining about it, I started building something else.

dw, i still complain about it, i'm french after all

Lacy, founder
Our values

Basic values,
somehow forgotten.

✦

Inclusion

Your gender, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, or disability is nobody's business but yours.

No one should have to wonder if they belong here. There's room for everyone.

◈

Ethics

We won't pretend constant drama, betting platforms, and communities built to extract money are just "how it is."

Not everything that works is worth building.

∞

Equity

Competing, learning, and being part of a community shouldn't depend on your wallet.

Not everything belongs behind a paywall.
