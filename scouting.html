The reality today

Built on Twitter. Not built for it.

Every LFP goes out as a tweet or a Discord message, sorted by nothing more than the order it was posted. There's no way to filter by role or region, no way to check who's actually played where, and once it's not pinned anymore, it's gone. Between a timeline, a dozen servers, and DMs spread across three apps, finding someone takes far longer than it should.
